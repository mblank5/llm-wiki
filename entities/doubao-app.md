# Doubao App (豆包)

豆包 (Doubao) is ByteDance's AI assistant app, serving hundreds of millions of users in China. It is the deployment platform for the [[seeduplex]] full-duplex speech model, where the "打电话" (phone call) feature provides native full-duplex voice interaction. Doubao represents one of the largest-scale deployments of end-to-end speech AI models.
