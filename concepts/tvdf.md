# TVDF (Temporal Video Distillation Framework)

TVDF (Temporal Video Distillation Framework) is a distillation framework focused on video understanding tasks, where temporal information plays a critical role. TVDF is referenced in the context of [[video-opd]], which applies on-policy distillation techniques to video temporal grounding tasks.
