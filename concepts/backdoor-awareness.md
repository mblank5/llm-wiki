# Backdoor Awareness

Backdoor awareness refers to the ability of large language models to detect or articulate the presence of backdoor triggers planted in their training data. This concept is related to [[behavioral-self-awareness]], as it requires the model to reason about its own learned behaviors — specifically, whether it has been conditioned to exhibit targeted misbehavior on specific triggers.

## Related

- [[behavioral-self-awareness]] — Backdoor awareness is a specific instance of behavioral self-awareness applied to planted triggers
- [[situational-awareness]] — Broader capability of understanding one's own state and context
