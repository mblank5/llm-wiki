# Situational Awareness

Situational awareness in AI systems refers to a model's ability to understand its own context, state, capabilities, and the broader environment in which it operates. This concept is related to [[behavioral-self-awareness]] but is broader, encompassing not just knowledge of learned behaviors but also understanding of deployment context, user intent, and system constraints. Situational awareness is a critical consideration in AI safety research.
