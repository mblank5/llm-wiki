# Introspection

Introspection in the context of large language models refers to the capability of an LLM to examine and report on its own internal decision-making processes and learned behaviors. This concept is central to [[behavioral-self-awareness]] research, where models demonstrate the ability to articulate systematic patterns in their behavior. The extent to which LLM introspection reflects genuine self-modeling versus correlated training effects remains an open question.
